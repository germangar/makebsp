**`BuildSurfaceAdjacencyGraph(entity_t *e)`**
- Iterates all surfaces within the entity.
- Compares vertex positions to find shared collinear chains (using `VectorCompare` with epsilon).
- Registers bidirectional `surfaceNeighbor_t` links.

**`ChamferSurfaceEdges(entity_t *e)` — Simultaneous Single-Pass Rebuild**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sequential chamfering (edge A, then edge B) is **geometrically invalid** at 3D corners. After edge A moves the shared corner vertex, edge B's inset is computed from a corrupted position, leaving a triangular hole. The simultaneous single-pass approach is the only correct method.

For each surface:
1. **Collect ALL edges** needing chamfering by walking `surfaceNeighbors[]` — without modifying any geometry.
2. **Compute ALL insets** via `ComputeAllInsets()` from the **original, untouched** vertex positions.
   - Simple vertices (on one chain only): perpendicular offset.
   - Corner vertices (on two chains): **mitre joint** — intersect the two inset lines in the face plane.
3. **Rebuild the Inner Body once** with all inset positions applied in a single pass.
4. **Spawn one Chamfer Strip** per chamfered edge.

**`static ComputeAllInsets()`**
- Iterates all collected edges to build an `insetMap[vertexIndex]` lookup.
- For single-edge vertices: `inset = xyz + cross(faceNormal, edgeDir) * CHAMFER_WIDTH`.
- For two-edge corner vertices: compute line-line intersection of the two inset lines to find the mitre point.
- Clamps mitre intersection distance to avoid runaway offsets at very acute angles.

**`static ApplyAllChamfers()`**
- Rebuilds Inner Body in-place: chain vertices → `insetMap` positions with `faceNormal`. Vertex count unchanged.
- Spawns one `AllocDrawSurf()` strip per edge: outer chain gets `blendedNormal`, inner chain gets `faceNormal`.

**`static IsChamferCandidate()`**
- Returns false for patches, misc_models, flare surfaces, NODRAW/SKY/NOLIGHTMAP shaders.
